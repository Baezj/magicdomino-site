Ten tables from around the world

Each with its real rules, researched and sourced, and an illustrated rulebook built from the game's own tiles. Pick your game — the words on the table follow it.

02 / Opponents

Computers that actually play

Easy, Medium and Hard are measured over thousands of hands, not guessed. Your partner always plays to win with you — and no computer ever peeks at your hand.

03 / Together

Play with real people

Sit down with friends on the same Wi-Fi, or send a six-letter code and play from anywhere. Or tap Quick Match and the app finds a stranger who plays your game.

Nearby · Anywhere · Quick Match
04 / Brackets

Tournaments, played out

Run a whole bracket against the computer or with friends, and watch the draw fill in as you win. On a big screen the bracket sits right beside the table.

05 / The calls

The calls you grew up with

Capicúa, the round pass, the pase de salida, the tranque — called on the table the instant the tile lands, with the fireworks they deserve. Points fly straight into the score.

06 / Feel

Made for the phone in your hand

Tap a tile, or drag it to either end. Every flight, slam and shake is real, and all of it honours Reduce Motion. Close the app mid-hand and pick up right where you left off.
