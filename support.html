Frequently Asked Questions

The scorekeeper

How does AI score counting work?
Point your camera at your dominoes using the camera icon in the app. Our AI will automatically detect and count the pips in real-time as the dominoes appear in view. Make sure the dominoes are clearly visible and well-lit for best results.
What's included in the free version?
The free version includes basic score tracking for 2-4 players and limited themes. You get 3 free AI counting uses per game, and you can watch a short video to unlock 3 more whenever you need them — or upgrade to Premium for unlimited AI counting with no ads. You can play unlimited games! The game (iOS 26+ and Android) is free to play too: free players may see an ad after a finished game, never during one. In the game, free players may also see a small banner while waiting alone in an online lobby, can watch an optional video to play their next 3 games without ads, and can watch one to try a Play pack free for 24 hours — never during a hand. Premium removes every ad.
What does Premium include?
Premium unlocks unlimited AI counting, ad-free experience, tournament mode, all themes and animations, game sharing, custom scoreboard backgrounds and saved Name Styles on both platforms (iOS 26+ & Android). In the game it removes the ads between games and includes every look in the game’s shop — tables, tiles, frames and celebrations. On iOS you also get iCloud sync, plus widgets & Live Activity on iOS 26 or later; these are not available on Android. Live Share & Transfer hosting is Premium on both iOS 26+ and Android; joining a shared game and using the Game Timer are free for everyone on both platforms. Recurring subscriptions (Monthly, 6-Month, Annual) include a 14-day free trial for eligible new subscribers; the App Store or Google Play shows it before you confirm. Lifetime Access requires immediate one-time payment with no trial.
Does the 14-day free trial apply to all Premium options?
No. The 14-day free trial comes only with the recurring subscriptions (Monthly, 6-Month and Annual) and only for eligible new subscribers — the App Store or Google Play shows the trial before you confirm. Lifetime Access is a one-time payment with no trial period, and gives you permanent Premium access.
How do I add widgets to my home screen? (Premium, iOS 26+ only)
Widgets are available on iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later. Long-press on your home screen, tap the "+" button in the top left, search for "Magic Domino", and select the widget size you want. You can add widgets to your home screen or lock screen to see your live scores! Widgets are not available on Android.
What is Live Activity? (Premium, iOS 26+ only)
Live Activity displays your current game score in real-time on your lock screen and Dynamic Island. When you start a new game with Premium, your live score automatically appears! It updates as you add points during the game.
How do I add Quick Points from my Lock Screen or Dynamic Island? (Premium, iOS 26+ only)
With Premium and iOS 26+, when you have an active game, you'll see Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Simply tap the Quick Points button to add points without opening the app. You can also undo your last score directly from there!
What are Quick Points, and how do I change them?
The +25 (or +30) button adds those points in one tap. Hold it to choose the amount and the style — Inline (inside the score field), Small or Big. You can also change them in Settings → Quick Points.
How do I customize my scoreboard background? (Premium, iOS 26+ & Android)
On iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later, and on Android, go to Settings → Scoreboard Background → "Add photo" (or pick one of the gradients). Choose a photo from your library, adjust the opacity and blur to your liking, and save! Your photo is stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
Why can't I see the custom background or widget features?
Both are Premium features, so first make sure you have an active Premium subscription. Beyond that:
  • Custom Backgrounds are available on Android and on iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later. Make sure your device meets these requirements.
  • Widgets & Live Activity require iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later. They are not available on Android.
How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Go to Settings → tap the Premium card → tap "Restore" at the top of the screen. On iOS your purchase is tied to your Apple ID; on Android it's tied to the Google account you use in Google Play. It will be restored automatically as long as you're signed in to the same account you purchased with. A purchase restores on devices that use the same store: one made in the App Store on iPhone and iPad, one made in Google Play on Android.
Can I sync my games across devices?
On iOS, yes — with a Premium subscription, your game history syncs automatically via iCloud across all your Apple devices. There is no cross-device sync on Android. On all platforms, your game history is always saved locally on your device, and Send a Copy moves a game or tournament to another phone — iPhone or Android.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On iOS, subscriptions are managed through Apple: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Magic Domino → Cancel Subscription. On Android, they're managed through Google Play: open Google Play → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Magic Domino → Cancel, or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
How do I turn off usage stats or change my ad choices?
Go to Settings → About → Share anonymous usage stats to turn the anonymous counts off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Settings → About → Privacy choices for ads lets you review or change your ad consent at any time.
How do I get a copy of my data or have it deleted?
Email support@magicdomino.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can delete your ranked profile yourself at Play → Ranked (also in Play settings), and deleting the app removes everything that is kept only on your phone. On Android you can also erase it without uninstalling: Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Storage → Clear storage. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How does Tournament Mode work?
Create a tournament with any number of teams from 2 to 32 — it does not have to be a round number. Pick your format: single elimination (one game decides each match), a best-of-3, 5, or 7 series, or double elimination, where a team has to lose twice before it is out. The app builds the bracket, works out the byes for you when the team count isn't a power of two, and tracks winners through every round until a champion is crowned!
Can I share my games with friends?
Yes, in three ways. On iOS 26 or later and on Android, tap the share button on the scoreboard: Share Live opens a room so others can follow the score on their own phones as you play, Send a Copy hands a game or tournament to another device, and Enter Code lets you join a game someone else is sharing. You can also share a game result as an image at any time. It works the same on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and you can share between them.
How does Live Share work? (iOS 26+ & Android — hosting needs Premium)
Start a game, tap the share button in the header, and choose Share Live. You'll get a short code and a QR code. Hosting a session needs Premium, but the people watching don't need it at all — anyone you give the code to can enter it — or scan the QR from the Counter screen — and watch your scoreboard update in real time on their own phone. No more leaning over to see someone else's screen. Everyone can follow along whether they're sitting at the same table or somewhere else entirely.
Does Live Share need an internet connection?
Not always. If everyone is on the same Wi-Fi network, phones connect to each other directly over that network and nothing goes through the internet at all — handy at a house with patchy service. When someone is somewhere else, the game is relayed through our server so it can reach them. The first time you use Live Share on the same network, iOS asks for Local Network permission and Android asks to find nearby devices. You can decline either one and Live Share will still work through the relay.
Who can see my game when I share it live?
Only people who have your share code. Treat it like a door key — anyone holding it can watch, so give it out only to people you want following along. Viewers can see the game as it appears on your scoreboard: team names, scores, round history, and the timer if you have it on. They can watch, but they can't change anything. Ending the session closes the room and stops all sharing immediately. Since team names are whatever you type in, use nicknames if you'd rather not share real ones. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
How do I show the bracket on a TV? (free, no app needed)
Any screen with a web browser can become the tournament board — a smart TV, a laptop on HDMI, or a spare phone. On that screen, open magicdomino.com, choose Watch Live, and type the 6-character room code from the host's share screen. That's it: no app to install, no account, and nothing for the viewer to pay. The page connects on its own, so the host's phone doesn't have to stay plugged in or even stay in the room — it can go back in a pocket and the board keeps updating. It follows your browser's language automatically and reads right-to-left in Arabic and Urdu. If you reload or the signal drops for a moment, the standings come straight back instead of flashing an empty screen.
How long does a share code last?
A live room stays open for as long as you keep the session running — close it and the code stops working. A code used to send a copy of a game is single-use: once the other device claims the game, the code is spent, and unclaimed codes expire on their own.
Why don't I see Live Share, Name Styles, or the Game Timer?
Live Share, Name Styles and the Game Timer are available on Android and on iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later. On iOS 17 or 18 the app works fully, but none of these appear.

Premium requirements differ: the Game Timer is free for everyone (and off by default — turn it on in Settings → Game Timer). Name Style presets are free; saving your own custom styles needs Premium. For Live Share, hosting a session or sending a copy needs Premium, but joining someone else's game with a code is always free.
How do I style team names? (iOS 26+ & Android — presets free, saving needs Premium)
Touch and hold a team name on the scoreboard to open the Name Style center. Pick one of the built-in presets, or touch and hold a preset to customize it — font, color, effect (glow, shadow, outline, or stacked), effect strength, and uppercase. The built-in presets are free; saving your own custom styles requires Premium. Everything is stored on your device.
What does the Game Timer show? (iOS 26+ & Android, free for everyone)
It's off by default; turn it on in Settings → Game Timer. Once on, a clock appears in the header showing how long the game has been going, starting from the first score added. In Tournament mode it also times each match and the tournament as a whole, including breaks between matches. Elapsed time appears on share cards too, so it's included when you share a game result. Timestamps are already saved with your games, so switching the timer on mid-game still shows the true elapsed time.
The AI isn't counting correctly. What should I do?
Make sure your dominoes are:
  • Well-lit and clearly visible in the camera view
  • Not overlapping each other
  • Against a contrasting background
  • Held steady for the AI to detect them
If issues persist, you can always manually enter the score.
Is my photo safe when I use a custom background?
Absolutely! When you select a photo for your scoreboard background, it's compressed and stored locally on your device only. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. You have complete control and can remove the custom background anytime.

The game

What is the game in Magic Domino, and where do I find it?
Magic Domino is two rooms in one app. The scorekeeper counts and keeps the score of the game you play with a real set at a real table. The game lets you play dominoes on the phone itself — against the computer, or with friends nearby or anywhere. Open the Play tab. The game runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and on Android 12 or later.
Which domino games can I play?
Ten tables, each with its own real rules: Dominican (the default), Puerto Rican, Classic, Cuban (double-nine, 55 tiles), Fives, Brazilian, Jamaican, Kozel, Turkish and Gaple. Pick a table from Table setup on the home screen — the words, the calls and the scoring on the table follow it. Tap the book in the corner for that table's illustrated rulebook, one card per rule, drawn with the game's own tiles.
How do I play a tile?
Tiles you can play glow on your rack. Tap one to play it when only one end of the line takes it. When both ends take it, both light up — tap the end you want, or drag the tile there. If you can't play, the knock happens by itself, because there is nothing to decide. At a table that draws from the pile, tap the pile to draw.
How good is the computer? Does it cheat?
It never cheats. No computer ever looks at your hand or at the pile — the Hard level works out what you might be holding from what has been played and who knocked on what, exactly like a strong player does. Easy, Medium and Hard are measured against each other over thousands of hands rather than guessed. When you play in partners, your partner plays at full strength at every level, because a teammate who sits on their doubles isn't an easier game, it's a worse one.
How do I play with friends or other people?
Open the Online card. Find a match seats you with people looking for the same game — one on one, two against two, or a free-for-all. If the table isn't full within a minute, computers take the empty seats (anyone seated can tap Play now sooner), and at most games three people can play a free-for-all on their own. Play with friends opens your table at once: tap Edit on the game card at the top to set the game, tap an empty chair to send the invite — friends tap the link and sit down — then tap Deal. Got a code? Tap Join beside Play with friends. To change which game Find a match looks for, tap Match setup under it. A friend’s table on the same Wi-Fi shows up on the Online card itself — tap Sit down.
How does an online table connect?
Friends on the same Wi-Fi connect straight to the host’s phone, and everyone else joins over the internet with the same code; if the internet is down, the table can open on the Wi-Fi alone. At a friends’ table, computers can fill the empty chairs once at least one other person has sat down. A small light — beside the word Online at the top of the table, and on the × during a game — shows the connection: green when the table is open or everyone is connected, amber while somebody is reconnecting, red if the table can’t be reached. The host’s phone runs the table. If it drops out, another player’s phone takes the table over within seconds and the game carries on; the host sits back down as a player when they return. A table opened on the Wi-Fi alone closes with its host, and everyone is told. Each turn at an online table has a time limit — at a friends’ table the host picks 15 or 30 seconds, or none. Run out and the computer plays that one move for you; miss two turns in a row and it keeps playing for you until you tap the screen.
What is the Daily Deal?
It’s on the Play home, under the cards. Every day, everyone gets the same tiles: a Dominican game to 100, you and a Hard computer partner against two Hard computers. Only your first game of the day counts — if you leave early, it counts as it stood, and a win always ranks above a loss. Tap Play it again to practise as often as you like; your counted score stays the same. Your score is your side’s points. Share it with a link, and if you use Game Center on iPhone or iPad, or Google Play Games on Android, open the Leaderboard to see where you rank today and earn achievements (in Game Center you can also Challenge friends to beat it). Game Center is Apple’s service and Google Play Games is Google’s: Magic Domino never stores your Game Center ID or your Play Games player ID. A new deal arrives every day at 04:00 UTC — midnight in the Dominican Republic. The Daily Deal is part of the game, which needs iOS 26 or later or Android 12 or later. Play it on consecutive days to build a streak, shown on the result sheet — only the first game of each day counts.
Do I need a name? Who sees it?
No. A name is optional, and you can play forever without one. Nobody sees a name at a computer table but you. Online, the people at your table see it — so pick something you're happy for strangers to read. Tap your own seat to set, change or remove it at any time. Names are checked before they are shown: no hate speech, slurs, harassment or impersonation. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
Someone at my table has an offensive name or is behaving badly. What can I do?
Tap the ⋯ beside their name — or, during a game, tap 💬 and then ⋯ on their row — and choose Report or Block. If it was a voice note or their photo, choose that as the reason: the note or photo goes with the report. Blocking is immediate and lives on your device: Quick Match will never seat the two of you together again. Reports reach us and are reviewed within 24 hours; offending names and photos are removed. You can also email support@magicdomino.com. Blocking works both ways — if someone blocks you, Quick Match keeps you apart too.
How do I mute people at an online table?
Tap 💬 at an online table. From there you can mute table talk, mute voice notes, or mute, report or block one player.
How do voice notes work?
At an online table with other people, hold the microphone button beside 💬 and talk — up to five seconds — then let go to send it; slide your finger away before letting go to cancel. Everyone at the table hears it once. To quiet one player, tap 💬 and mute them; Mute voice notes there quiets everyone at that table, and Play settings → Voice notes from others turns them off on your phone at every table. The host of a friends' table can switch voice notes off for the whole table. Voice notes are for players 13 and up, follow your phone's silent switch, and are not kept — unless another player reports one, in which case that note is kept for 30 days so we can review it. They work on iPhone, iPad and Android, and the first time you record one your phone asks for microphone permission.
Can I use my own photo at the table?
Yes, on iOS 26 or later and on Android. Tap your seat — or your name on the Play home — then Use a photo to choose one or take one. It is checked automatically before anyone sees it: no nudity, violence, hate symbols, drugs, rude gestures or writing. Photos are for players 13 and up, and you need an internet connection to add one. Remove photo takes it down at once, and the other players see one of the built-in faces instead. Details in our Privacy Policy.
What can I buy in the game's shop?
Tap the shop button at the top of the Play home. Packs change how the game looks — Tables & Tiles, Frames & Emblems and Celebrations, or all three as The Collection, which costs less. They are one-time purchases, and nothing you buy changes the rules or helps you win. Tap Try free for 24 hours to watch a short video and wear a pack for a day first. Premium includes every pack. Bought one on another device with the same store account? Tap Restore purchases in the shop.
Where are the game's settings?
Tap the gear on the Play home (or go to Settings → Play settings). There you can set the Scoreboard size (Compact · Standard — during a game you can also hold the scoreboard at the top of the table to change it), the Game speed (Normal · Relaxed — the computer's pace), Slam effects, Sounds, Haptics, whether you hear voice notes from others, and your blocked players.
What is Ranked?
Ranked gives you one number that moves when you win or lose a game in Find a match against other people. Your first five games place you; after that, every game moves your number up or down, and the bar under it shows how far you are from the next hundred. Only games where every chair is a ranked person count — a table with a computer, or friends you invited, plays as usual. Ranked needs Game Center on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Games on Android: sign in to it, and your number follows you to any device signed in to the same account. iPhone and Android players share one ladder. Open it from the trophy on the Online card or from Play settings → Ranked, where you can see the top players and delete your ranked profile at any time. On iPhone and iPad, players whose Apple account says they are under 13 are not ranked. Details in our Privacy Policy.
Is the game available on Android?
Yes. The game runs on Android 12 or later, as well as on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and iPhone and Android players sit at the same tables — with friends, over the Wi-Fi, in Find a match, in Ranked and in the Daily Deal. Voice notes, your own photo and Ranked work on Android too; on Android, Ranked, achievements and the Daily Deal leaderboard use Google Play Games instead of Game Center. The shop sells every pack on Android too. The scorekeeper works on both too, and a Live Share between an iPhone and an Android phone works as it always has.
Do the games I play count in History? What if I close the app mid-game?
Finished games are filed in History with a Play badge, right beside the games you scored at a real table; tap one to see its final card again. Walking out of a game mid-hand files nothing. If you close the app during a game against the computer, the next time you open the Play tab it asks whether you want to pick up where you left off. An online table stays open while people are at it: if your app closes, you are seated back at your table when you reopen it; if the host drops out or leaves, another phone at the table takes over as host and the game carries on; the table ends when everybody has left.

Android

Which Android phones can run Magic Domino?
Any phone or tablet with Android 12 or later and Google Play. Get it on Google Play. The scorekeeper, the AI Counter, tournaments, Live Share, the game — with voice notes, your own photo and Ranked — and the Daily Deal all work on Android, and iPhone and Android players share live scores and sit at the same tables. On Android, Google Play Games takes the place of Game Center, and the shop sells every pack. Only on iPhone and iPad: iCloud sync, widgets and Live Activity, and Game Center.
Which permissions does the Android app ask for?
Camera — for the AI Counter, for scanning a share code's QR, and if you take a photo for your face in the game. Pictures for counting are processed on the phone and never saved or uploaded. Microphone — only when you record a voice note at an online table. Local network (Android 17 and later) — to find Live Share rooms and game tables on your Wi-Fi; decline it and they connect through our server instead. Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker, so the app never sees the rest of your photos. To change a permission later, open Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Permissions.
A magicdomino.com link opens in the browser instead of the app. What can I do?
Game invitations and Live Share links (magicdomino.com/j/…) and Daily Deal links (magicdomino.com/d/…) open Magic Domino directly when the app is allowed to open them. If one opens in the browser, go to Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Open by default, turn on Open supported links, and tick every magicdomino.com link listed there. You can always type the code instead: the share button on the scoreboard → Enter Code, or Join on the Play tab's Online card.
How do I get Premium back on a new Android phone?
Sign in to Google Play on the new phone with the Google account that bought Premium, install Magic Domino, then go to Settings → tap the Premium card → Restore Purchase. A subscription bought in Google Play restores on Android, and one bought in the App Store restores on iPhone and iPad; each store keeps its own purchases. Your game history stays on the old phone — use Send a Copy to move a game or tournament across.
Can my friend and I team up against other players?
Yes. Open your table, invite your friend and choose 2 vs 2 — your friend sits across from you as your partner. Then tap Find opponents: your table joins the search, the two players who come sit as the other team, and the game deals itself once everybody is there. If nobody comes within a minute, computers take the open chairs (or tap Deal with computers at any time). Stop searching makes the table private again. If another pair is searching for the same game, the app may move you and your friend to their table together — you always stay partners. After the game, the host can tap Play Again: everybody goes back to the same table, still seated, and can search again. Moving together needs the latest version of the app on both phones; otherwise the other players come to your table instead.
