Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Privacy is important to us, and we've designed Magic Domino with your data security in mind. Magic Domino™ has no accounts, and we never ask for your email or contact details. Your games are stored on your device. The exceptions are the features that connect devices, and each does nothing until you start it: if you share a scoreboard live, its contents are sent to the devices you share with — see Live Share & Transfer — and if you play the game with other people, your moves, the name you chose and, if you add them, your photo and voice notes reach the people at your table — see Playing the Game Online. If you play Ranked, our server keeps your rating with a scrambled code made from your Game Center ID (iPhone and iPad) or your Google Play Games player ID (Android), never the ID itself — see Ranked. The app also sends us anonymous usage counts, with no identifier for you or your device, which you can turn off — see Anonymous Usage Statistics.

Information We Collect

Magic Domino is designed to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your email, phone number or address. Below is everything the app does with your information, feature by feature; every feature that sends anything off your device is optional.

  • Game Data: All scores, game history, and tournament results are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data.
  • iCloud Sync (Premium, iOS only — optional): On iPhone and iPad, if you enable iCloud sync, your game data is stored in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple. We do not have access to your iCloud data. There is no cross-device sync on Android: your games stay on the phone, and Send a Copy moves a game or tournament to another device.
  • Camera Access: Used for AI score counting, to scan a share code's QR, and — only if you choose to — to take a photo for your face in the game. For counting, all image processing happens on your device in real time, and the picture is never saved or uploaded. On Android, the app asks for camera permission the first time you use the camera, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Permissions. A photo you take for your face is handled as described under Your Photo in the Game.
  • Photo Library Access (Premium, iOS 26+ and Android): Premium users on iOS 26 or later and on Android can select photos from their library to customize the scoreboard background. Selected photos are compressed, stored locally on your device, and never uploaded to our servers. We do not have access to your photo library or the photos you select.
  • Widgets & Live Activity (Premium, iOS 26+): Premium users on iOS 26 or later can display game scores on their home screen, lock screen, and Dynamic Island via widgets and Live Activity. You can also add Quick Points and undo scores directly from your Lock Screen. All widget and Live Activity data is read from and written to your locally stored game data and is never transmitted to our servers. Widgets are not available on Android. You can also add points by voice with Siri: the request is handled by Apple's Siri, and the points are written to the game on your device — nothing is sent to our servers.
  • Live Share & Transfer (iOS 26+ and Android — hosting requires Premium, joining is free): These features are entirely optional and do nothing until you start them. When you share a game live, or send a copy to another device, the game's contents — team names, scores, round history, tournament titles and brackets, elapsed time, and the display name shown to viewers — are transmitted so the other devices can show them. Whenever both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, this happens directly between them over your local network and never reaches us. When a direct connection isn't possible, the data is relayed through our server at live.magicdomino.com so it can reach a device somewhere else. Anyone holding the share code can view the game, so only give the code to people you want watching. A live room exists only while you keep it open, and a transfer code is single-use and expires. We don't use game content for anything other than delivering it to the devices you're sharing with, and we don't combine it with anything else. Team names are free text you type yourself — if you'd rather not send real names, use nicknames. Like any internet service, our relay receives the IP address of connecting devices as an unavoidable part of how network connections work. Live Share works the same way on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and shares between them.
  • Local Network Access: Live Share and the game's Wi-Fi tables look for other devices on the same Wi-Fi network so they can connect directly instead of going through our relay. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time you use it; Android 17 and later ask for Android's local network permission (earlier Android versions do not ask). This access is used only to find and connect to devices you are actively sharing or playing with — never to scan your network for anything else, and never to determine your location. You can decline it, and Live Share and online tables will use the relay instead.
  • Watching in a Browser (magicdomino.com — free, no app and no account): A tournament can also be followed in an ordinary web browser — on a phone, a laptop, or a smart TV — by opening magicdomino.com and entering the room code. Nothing needs to be installed. That page holds its own connection to our relay, so like any website it receives the IP address of the connecting device as an unavoidable part of how network connections work. We set no cookies on it, and there are no tracking pixels or advertising anywhere on magicdomino.com. To see which pages are useful, each page view on magicdomino.com is counted anonymously — which page, the browser's language, and whether a store button was tapped — and so is the tournament board: whether it reached a live tournament or the code did not work, whether it was opened by typing a code or from an invitation link, the kind of screen as the browser describes itself (TV, computer, tablet or phone), roughly how long it stayed open, and whether it was still open when a champion was crowned. All of it is counted with no cookie, no identifier, and nothing stored about you; if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted at all. The page stores three things in your browser's own local storage, on your device, which we cannot read: your light-or-dark preference, your language choice if you have picked one with the language selector, and a copy of the bracket you are watching, saved under that room code. The bracket copy is what lets the page show you the standings again immediately if you reload or briefly lose signal instead of flashing an empty screen. It stays in your browser until you clear your browsing data for this site, and it contains the same game contents the host chose to share — team names, scores, and the bracket. Apart from those anonymous counts, nothing you do as a viewer is sent anywhere except the room code needed to join.
  • Playing the Game Online (iOS 26+ and Android — optional): A game against the computer stays on your device: the only thing sent is an anonymous count — which table and level, and whether it was won, never the tiles, moves or names — described under Anonymous Usage Statistics. When you sit at a table with other people, what reaches them is what a table needs: the display name you chose (or none — a name is optional), the moves of the game, the preset reactions you tap (emoji and short calls from a fixed list — never typed text), the table's rules and the room code, plus a random identifier your app generated once and keeps, which has no account behind it and tells us nothing about you. A table you open for friends is held on our server at live.magicdomino.com so they can join from anywhere, and friends on the same Wi-Fi also reach the host's phone directly; while the server holds the table, what the table shares passes through it, even when everyone sits on the same Wi-Fi. The host's phone, not our server, runs the game. So that the game can go on if the host's phone drops out, the host's phone also leaves the server a copy of the game in progress — the same names, moves and rules the players already see — which the next player's phone picks up to carry the table on; we never read it. The server keeps the table and that copy only while the table is in use, and deletes both within 12 hours of the last player leaving. A table opened on the Wi-Fi alone, when the internet cannot be reached, travels directly between the phones and never reaches us. Quick Match tables always go through the server. While you wait in Quick Match, a waiting list holds a per-search random id, your identifier and the identifiers of players you have blocked for at most sixty seconds at a time, so the two of you are never seated together. Like any internet service, the relay receives the IP address of connecting devices as an unavoidable part of how connections work. Names are free text you type yourself and are filtered on your device before they are shown to anyone. The relay also keeps a running count of how many rooms are open and how many devices are connected to them — totals only, never who. iPhone, iPad and Android players sit at the same tables, and all of this works the same way on each.
  • Voice Notes at an Online Table (iOS 26+ and Android — optional): At a table with other people you can hold the microphone button to send a voice note of up to five seconds. It is recorded only while you hold the button, sent through our server at live.magicdomino.com (or directly over the same Wi-Fi) to the players at your table, and played there; everyone at the table hears it, and in Find a match that includes people you do not know. We do not keep voice notes: the server passes them on and forgets them. The one exception is a note another player reports: that player's app sends us the note it received, we keep it for 30 days so we can review the report, and then it is deleted. You can mute one player's voice notes or all of them at any time, and the host of a friends' table can switch voice notes off for the whole table. The phone asks for microphone permission the first time you record (on Android, you can withdraw it in Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Permissions). Voice notes are for players 13 and up; on iPhone and iPad they are not available to anyone whose Apple account says they are under 13.
  • Your Photo in the Game (iOS 26+ and Android — optional, 13 and up): In the game you can wear your own photo instead of one of the built-in faces, chosen from your library (on Android, with Android's photo picker, so the app never sees the rest of your photos) or taken with the camera. Your phone crops it to a small square and first checks, on the phone itself, that it has no writing in it (on Android with Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on the device and sends nothing anywhere). It then uploads only that square to our photo server at avatars.magicdomino.com; hidden details stored in the image, such as location and camera data, are removed. Before anyone sees it, it is checked automatically by two services — Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety and Amazon Rekognition — which only answer whether it may be shown; nothing else about you is sent with it. A photo that passes is stored under a random code and shown from photos.magicdomino.com to the players at your tables. Anyone who has that code can see it, so only use a photo you are happy for strangers to see. Tap Remove photo at any time and we delete our copy; a photo whose app has not checked in for 90 days — for example because the app was deleted — is deleted as well. Other players can report your photo, and a photo that breaks the rules is removed. On iPhone and iPad, photos are not available to anyone whose Apple account says they are under 13.
  • Game Center (iPhone and iPad) and Google Play Games (Android) — optional: If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center on iPhone or iPad, or to Google Play Games on Android, the game sends it your Daily Deal score and the achievements you earn, so it can show leaderboards and achievements (and, in Game Center, challenges). Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and Google Play Games by Google under Google's privacy policy. Magic Domino never stores your Game Center ID or your Play Games player ID; the only time the app sends either to us is to prove who you are for Ranked, described below. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent, and the Daily Deal works the same.
  • Age Range (iOS 26+): Photos and voice notes are for players 13 and up. On iPhone and iPad, the first time you use one of them, the app asks Apple's Declared Age Range for an age range — never your date of birth — and keeps only the answer, on your device. It is never sent to us. If no age range is shared, both features stay available. The Android app does not ask for an age range.
  • Ranked (iOS 26+ and Android — optional, needs Game Center or Google Play Games): If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), games you play in Find a match against other people can move a rating. To recognise you as the same player on any phone, the app sends our server at live.magicdomino.com a one-time proof: on iPhone and iPad, a proof signed by Apple together with your Game Center player ID; on Android, a one-time code from Google Play Games, which our server exchanges with Google to learn your Play Games player ID. We check the proof and keep only a scrambled code made from that ID with a secret key, never the ID itself. We then give you a random ranked ID, and keep with it: the Play name and built-in face you chose, your rating, your best rating, your current run of wins, your number of games and wins, and how many of your games ended with reports that did not agree (so results cannot be quietly cancelled). Your name, face and rating are shown on the public ranked top table, which anyone using the app can see, and to players at ranked tables. iPhone and Android players are rated on the same ladder. To work out a game's result, the server briefly keeps the outcome without anyone's ID for two days, and how many times two players met that day so the same pair cannot pile up points. None of this is used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can delete your ranked profile at any time in Play → Ranked (also in Play settings) → Delete my ranked profile (the Ranked page also opens from the trophy on the Online card); your rating, history and the link to your Game Center or Play Games account are erased at once. For 30 days afterwards we keep only the scrambled code made from your player ID and the date, so a deleted profile cannot be re-created straight away to reset a rating; then it is deleted too. On iPhone and iPad, players whose Apple account says they are under 13, or who may not play multiplayer games, are not ranked.
  • Announcements and Update Notices (iOS 26+ and Android): Now and then the app downloads a small public file of announcements from our server at stats.magicdomino.com — the same file for everyone. The request carries no identifier, so we cannot tell who downloaded it; like any internet request it reveals your IP address, which is used only to deliver the file and is not stored. Your phone decides which announcement applies to you — by platform, app version, language, the Region in your settings and whether you have Premium — and remembers, on your phone, the ones you closed. If you share anonymous usage stats, the app also counts how many times each announcement was shown, tapped or closed, as totals only. To tell you about a new version, the app asks Apple's App Store which version is current (on iPhone and iPad) or uses Google Play's in-app update service (on Android).
  • Reports and Blocking: Blocking a player is a list kept on your device; we never see it except as the identifiers sent with a Quick Match search. The same happens when a table you sit at looks for players: your phone hands those identifiers to the phone hosting the table, so its search keeps you apart from them too, and they are dropped when you leave. When you report a player, we keep the reported player's identifier and their name as it appeared, the reason you chose, your own identifier, the room code and the time — and, if you report a voice note or a photo, that note or photo, as described above. We keep it so we can review it within 24 hours and remove offending content; the report log keeps only the most recent reports — a few hundred — and older ones are deleted automatically, and a voice note attached to a report is deleted after 30 days. None of it is tied to an account, because there are none.
  • Anonymous Usage Statistics (iPhone, iPad and Android — you can turn it off): To learn which features matter and what needs fixing, the app sends us counts of what happens in it — for example that a game was finished, which mode and target score it used, which page was opened, how long the app stayed open (and, while the app is on screen, a note every five minutes that it is open and which page is showing, so we can see how many people are using it at the same time), the app and system version, the device type (phone or tablet), the app's language, the Region set in your phone's settings, whether accessibility settings such as larger text are on, and crash and performance reports — from Apple's MetricKit on iPhone and iPad, and on Android the reason the app last closed (a crash or a freeze), as Android records it on the phone. These counts contain no name, no team or player name, nothing you type, no photos, no location, no advertising identifier, and no identifier for you or your device — the app never creates one. Each count carries only the day it happened (the five-minute note also its five-minute window) and the day the app was first opened, so we can tell how many players come back without being able to tell any of them apart. Counts are sent to our server at stats.magicdomino.com, which keeps only totals: per day, and for the last 48 hours per five minutes; the address your connection comes from is used to deliver the request and is never stored. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → About → “Share anonymous usage stats”; anything not yet sent is deleted from your device immediately.
  • Name Styles (iOS 26+ and Android — saving custom styles requires Premium): Fonts, colors, and effects you apply to team names are stored locally on your device as part of your app settings. They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Game Timer (iOS 26+ and Android, free for everyone): Off by default. When enabled, elapsed time is calculated on your device from timestamps already saved with your games. Nothing extra is collected, and nothing is transmitted — though elapsed time does appear on share cards and in shared games, as part of that game's contents. Separately, the anonymous usage counts (see Anonymous Usage Statistics) include how long a finished game lasted, rounded to the minute, with no scores or names.
  • Purchase Information: All subscriptions and purchases are managed securely by Apple through the App Store (on iOS) or by Google through Google Play Billing (on Android). We do not store or have access to your payment details.
  • Advertising (Free Users Only): Free version users may see ads served through Google AdMob, with Unity Ads competing for the same ad spaces through AdMob mediation. In the game, ads appear only after a finished game, while you wait alone for players to arrive, or when you choose to watch one — never during a hand. These networks collect certain device information to choose, show and measure ads (on iOS the app never asks to track you; on Android they may use the device's Advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings). Premium users have an ad-free experience with no ad tracking.

How We Use Your Information

We have no accounts, and the only statistics we keep are anonymous daily totals, so there is very little to use. Almost everything stays on your device.

  • Game data remains on your device to provide score tracking and history
  • The camera counts dominoes in real time on your phone, and nothing is uploaded; only when you choose, it is also used to take a photo for your face in the game
  • Selected background photos are stored locally on your device only (iOS 26+ and Android)
  • Widget and Live Activity data is read from and written to your locally stored game data (iOS 26+)
  • Game contents are transmitted only when you start Live Share or Transfer, and only to the devices you share with
  • Name Styles and Game Timer settings stay on your device
  • iCloud sync (if enabled by you on iOS) uses Apple's secure infrastructure
  • When you play the game with other people, your name, photo, voice notes and moves go only to the people at your table (and, if you play Ranked, your name, face and rating also appear on the ranked top table); a Quick Match search is held only while you wait; the report log keeps only the most recent reports — a few hundred — and older ones are deleted automatically, and a voice note attached to a report is deleted after 30 days
  • Anonymous usage counts (which features are used, crashes, app versions) are used only to decide what to improve and fix in Magic Domino. They are never sold, never shared, never used for advertising, and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Your Game Center or Google Play Games identity is used only to recognise you for Ranked ratings; announcements and update notices are chosen on your phone; and a photo you choose for your face is checked automatically before anyone sees it

Data Storage and Security

All your game data, custom background photos, name styles, and app settings are stored locally on your device in the app's secure storage. On iOS, if you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's secure infrastructure. We do not have access to your iCloud data or any files stored on your device.

Live Share and Transfer are the exception, and only while you are using them. Traffic to and from our relay is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). A live room holds the game only for as long as the session is open; a transfer payload is held only until it is claimed or expires. Neither is tied to an account, because there are no accounts. If you never use Live Share or Transfer, the contents of your games — team names, scores and rounds — never reach our servers; the only thing that does is the anonymous usage counts described under Anonymous Usage Statistics, which you can turn off.

If you watch a tournament in a browser, your browser also keeps its own copy of the bracket you were watching, on your device, so the page can repaint instantly on reload — see Watching in a Browser above. Clearing your browsing data for magicdomino.com removes it.

The game's online tables follow the same rules as Live Share: encrypted in transit, held only while the table is open, tied to no account. What remains after a table closes is only what is described above: a report you file (with a reported voice note, for 30 days), your photo for as long as you use it, and the copy of a game in progress that lets another player's phone take a table over, which is deleted within 12 hours of the last player leaving.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete your game data at any time from within the app settings
  • Remove custom background photos at any time from the background settings (iOS 26+ and Android)
  • Enable or disable iCloud sync at your preference (iOS)
  • Enable or disable widgets and Live Activity at your preference (iOS 26+)
  • Choose whether to use Live Share or Transfer at all — nothing is transmitted unless you start a session
  • End a live session at any time, which closes the room and stops all sharing
  • Use nicknames instead of real names for teams if you plan to share games
  • Decline local network permission and still use Live Share and online tables via the relay
  • On iOS, ads are never based on tracking you across other apps and websites — the app does not ask for that permission. On Android, you can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the phone)
  • Clear your browsing data for magicdomino.com to remove anything the web board stored in your browser
  • Play the game without a name, or set, change or remove your name at any time from your seat
  • Block a player so Quick Match never seats you together, and report a name or behaviour from the table
  • Turn off anonymous usage statistics at any time in the app: Settings → About → “Share anonymous usage stats”.
  • Remove your photo at any time — we delete our copy — or play with one of the built-in faces
  • Mute one player's voice notes or everyone's at the table, or turn voice notes from others off in Play settings; you are recorded only while you hold the microphone button
  • Delete your ranked profile at any time in Play → Ranked (also in Play settings) — your rating and the link to your Game Center or Google Play Games account are erased at once
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, change your ad choices at any time in Settings → About → Privacy choices for ads
  • On Android, turn camera, microphone or local network access off at any time in Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Permissions
  • On Android, erase everything the app keeps on your phone with Settings → Apps → Magic Domino → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling it; a purchase stays with your Google account and comes back with Restore Purchase
  • Uninstall the app to completely remove all locally stored data from your device
