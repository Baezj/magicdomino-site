Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Privacy is important to us, and we've designed Magic Domino with your data security in mind. Magic Domino™ has no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email, or contact details. Your games are stored on your device. The exceptions are the features that connect devices, and each does nothing until you start it: if you share a scoreboard live, its contents are sent to the devices you share with — see Live Share & Transfer — and if you play the game with other people, your moves and the name you chose reach the people at your table — see Playing the Game Online.

Information We Collect

Magic Domino is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from our users.

  • Game Data: All scores, game history, and tournament results are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data.
  • iCloud Sync (Premium, iOS only — optional): On iPhone and iPad, if you enable iCloud sync, your game data is stored in your personal iCloud account managed by Apple. We do not have access to your iCloud data. Cross-device sync is not yet available on Android and is planned for a future update.
  • Camera Access: Used only for AI score counting. All image processing happens locally on your device in real-time. Photos are never saved, stored, or uploaded anywhere.
  • Photo Library Access (Premium, iOS 26+ and Android): Premium users on iOS 26 or later and on Android can select photos from their library to customize the scoreboard background. Selected photos are compressed, stored locally on your device, and never uploaded to our servers. We do not have access to your photo library or the photos you select.
  • Widgets & Live Activity (Premium, iOS 26+): Premium users on iOS 26 or later can display game scores on their home screen, lock screen, and Dynamic Island via widgets and Live Activity. You can also add Quick Points and undo scores directly from your Lock Screen. All widget and Live Activity data is read from and written to your locally stored game data and is never transmitted to our servers. Widgets are not yet available on Android and are planned for a future update.
  • Live Share & Transfer (iOS 26+ and Android — hosting requires Premium, joining is free): These features are entirely optional and do nothing until you start them. When you share a game live, or send a copy to another device, the game's contents — team names, scores, round history, tournament titles and brackets, elapsed time, and the display name shown to viewers — are transmitted so the other devices can show them. Whenever both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, this happens directly between them over your local network and never reaches us. When a direct connection isn't possible, the data is relayed through our server at live.magicdomino.com so it can reach a device somewhere else. Anyone holding the share code can view the game, so only give the code to people you want watching. A live room exists only while you keep it open, and a transfer code is single-use and expires. We don't use game content for anything other than delivering it to the devices you're sharing with, and we don't combine it with anything else. Team names are free text you type yourself — if you'd rather not send real names, use nicknames. Like any internet service, our relay receives the IP address of connecting devices as an unavoidable part of how network connections work. Live Share works the same way on iPhone, iPad, and Android, and shares between them.
  • Local Network Access: Live Share looks for other devices on the same Wi-Fi network so they can connect directly instead of going through our relay. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time you use it; Android asks for the equivalent nearby-devices permission. This access is used only to find and connect to devices you are actively sharing with — never to scan your network for anything else, and never to determine your location. You can decline it, and Live Share will use the relay instead.
  • Watching in a Browser (magicdomino.com — free, no app and no account): A tournament can also be followed in an ordinary web browser — on a phone, a laptop, or a smart TV — by opening magicdomino.com and entering the room code. Nothing needs to be installed. That page holds its own connection to our relay, so like any website it receives the IP address of the connecting device as an unavoidable part of how network connections work. We set no cookies on it, and we run no analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising anywhere on magicdomino.com. The page stores three things in your browser's own local storage, on your device, which we cannot read: your light-or-dark preference, your language choice if you have picked one with the language selector, and a copy of the bracket you are watching, saved under that room code. The bracket copy is what lets the page show you the standings again immediately if you reload or briefly lose signal instead of flashing an empty screen. It stays in your browser until you clear your browsing data for this site, and it contains the same game contents the host chose to share — team names, scores, and the bracket. Nothing you do as a viewer is sent anywhere except the room code needed to join.
  • Playing the Game Online (iOS 26+ — optional): Games against the computer never leave your device. When you sit at a table with other people, what reaches them is what a table needs: the display name you chose (or none — a name is optional), the moves of the game, the table's rules and the room code, plus a random identifier your app generated once and keeps, which has no account behind it and tells us nothing about you. On a Nearby table on the same Wi-Fi this travels directly between the phones and never reaches us. On an Anywhere table, and in Quick Match, it is relayed through our server at live.magicdomino.com, which holds the table only while the host keeps it open and keeps nothing afterwards; the host's phone, not our server, runs the game. While you wait in Quick Match, a waiting list holds a per-search random id, your identifier and the identifiers of players you have blocked for at most sixty seconds at a time, so the two of you are never seated together. Like any internet service, the relay receives the IP address of connecting devices as an unavoidable part of how connections work. Names are free text you type yourself and are filtered on your device before they are shown to anyone.
  • Reports and Blocking: Blocking a player is a list kept on your device; we never see it except as the identifiers sent with a Quick Match search. Reporting a player is the one thing we keep after a table closes: the reported player's identifier and their name as it appeared, the reason you chose, your own identifier, the room code and the time. We keep it so we can review it within 24 hours and remove an offending name; a bounded number of recent reports is held and older ones fall away. None of it is tied to an account, because there are none.
  • Name Styles (iOS 26+ — saving custom styles requires Premium): Fonts, colors, and effects you apply to team names are stored locally on your device as part of your app settings. They are never uploaded to our servers. Name Styles are not yet available on Android and are planned for a future update.
  • Game Timer (iOS 26+ and Android, free for everyone): Off by default. When enabled, elapsed time is calculated on your device from timestamps already saved with your games. Nothing extra is collected, and nothing is transmitted — though elapsed time does appear on share cards and in shared games, as part of that game's contents.
  • Purchase Information: All subscriptions and purchases are managed securely by Apple through the App Store (on iOS) or by Google through Google Play (on Android). We do not store or have access to your payment details.
  • Advertising (Free Users Only): Free version users may see ads served through Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as a secondary network via AdMob mediation. These networks collect certain device information for ad personalization. Premium users have an ad-free experience with no ad tracking.

How We Use Your Information

We have no accounts and no analytics, so there is very little to use. Almost everything stays on your device.

  • Game data remains on your device to provide score tracking and history
  • Camera is used in real-time for AI counting only
  • Selected background photos are stored locally on your device only (iOS 26+ and Android)
  • Widget and Live Activity data is read from and written to your locally stored game data (iOS 26+)
  • Game contents are transmitted only when you start Live Share or Transfer, and only to the devices you share with
  • Name Styles and Game Timer settings stay on your device
  • iCloud sync (if enabled by you on iOS) uses Apple's secure infrastructure
  • When you play the game with other people, your name and moves go only to the people at your table; a Quick Match search is held only while you wait, and a report only until it has been reviewed

Data Storage and Security

All your game data, custom background photos, name styles, and app settings are stored locally on your device in the app's secure storage. On iOS, if you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's secure infrastructure. We do not have access to your iCloud data or any files stored on your device.

Live Share and Transfer are the exception, and only while you are using them. Traffic to and from our relay is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). A live room holds the game only for as long as the session is open; a transfer payload is held only until it is claimed or expires. Neither is tied to an account, because there are no accounts. If you never use Live Share or Transfer, none of your game data reaches our servers at any point.

If you watch a tournament in a browser, your browser also keeps its own copy of the bracket you were watching, on your device, so the page can repaint instantly on reload — see Watching in a Browser above. Clearing your browsing data for magicdomino.com removes it.

The game's online tables follow the same rules as Live Share: encrypted in transit, held only while the table is open, tied to no account. A report you file is the one thing we keep after a table closes, so we can act on it.

Third-Party Services

Magic Domino integrates with the following services:

  • Apple App Store (iOS): Handles all purchases and subscriptions securely on iPhone and iPad
  • Google Play (Android): Handles all purchases and subscriptions securely on Android devices
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage and validate subscriptions and purchases and to deliver Premium access consistently across iOS and Android. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription details (such as the product purchased, transaction receipt, and platform) along with an anonymous app-generated identifier. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment card details — those remain with Apple or Google. For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iCloud (iOS): Optional sync feature for Premium users — data stays in your personal iCloud account
  • Magic Domino Live Relay (live.magicdomino.com): Our own relay, operated by us and not a third party. It passes shared game contents between devices that cannot reach each other directly. It is used only during Live Share and Transfer sessions you start yourself.
  • The same relay carries the game's online tables (Anywhere and Quick Match) and receives the reports described above. Nearby tables never touch it.
  • Google AdMob (Free Users Only): We use Google AdMob as our primary advertising network to serve ads to free users. AdMob may collect and use the following information:
    • Device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
    • IP address
    • Device type and operating system
    • App usage and interaction data
    • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

    For more information on Google AdMob's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

  • Unity Ads (Free Users Only): Unity Ads serves as a secondary advertising network through AdMob mediation when AdMob does not fill an ad. Unity Ads may collect and use the same categories of information listed above (advertising identifiers, IP address, device type and operating system, app usage data, and ad interaction data).

    This data is used to show personalized ads and measure ad performance. Premium subscribers have zero ads and zero ad tracking.

    For more information on Unity Ads' data practices, please visit: Unity Ads Privacy Policy

We do not collect personal information such as your name or email, and we do not sell user data. Google AdMob and Unity Ads collect device data directly from your device for advertising purposes only (free users). RevenueCat receives only anonymous purchase and subscription data needed to deliver your Premium access. Apple and Google handle all payment information directly.

Children's Privacy

Magic Domino does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed to be family-friendly and safe for all ages.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete your game data at any time from within the app settings
  • Remove custom background photos at any time from the background settings (iOS 26+ and Android)
  • Enable or disable iCloud sync at your preference (iOS)
  • Enable or disable widgets and Live Activity at your preference (iOS 26+)
  • Choose whether to use Live Share or Transfer at all — nothing is transmitted unless you start a session
  • End a live session at any time, which closes the room and stops all sharing
  • Use nicknames instead of real names for teams if you plan to share games
  • Decline local network / nearby-devices permission and still use Live Share via the relay
  • Opt out of personalized ads by limiting ad tracking in your device settings (on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID)
  • Clear your browsing data for magicdomino.com to remove anything the web board stored in your browser
  • Play the game without a name, or set, change or remove your name at any time from your seat
  • Block a player so Quick Match never seats you together, and report a name or behaviour from the table
  • Uninstall the app to completely remove all locally stored data from your device
